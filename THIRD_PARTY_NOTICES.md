## County age add-on

Add-on contributor: **Mitch Zhang**, confirmed by Brandon Santor. Age data are U.S. Census Bureau 2020–2024 ACS five-year Detailed Tables B01001 (Sex by Age) and B01002 (Median Age by Sex): https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/groups/B01001.html and https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5/groups/B01002.html . COLE application development, corrections and integration: **Brandon Santor**. The original handoff is preserved in the source checkout. COLE uses published median-age estimates/MOEs and exact sums of male/female age-cell estimates divided by total population for the seven age-group shares. Shares are displayed rounded; filtering uses the unrounded ratio. Derived group-count/share MOEs are not calculated. The feature-owned read-only SQLite and manifest preserve source tokens, coverage and fingerprints. This source 0.8.0 integration does not update prior portable releases or claim Census Bureau endorsement.

## Greenspace add-on

**Margaret Worsham** contributed the Greenspace add-on. **Brandon Santor** receives credit for data curation, screening, validation, application development, corrections and integration. Data publishers: U.S. Geological Survey, PAD-US 4.1, and U.S. Census Bureau, full 2021 county boundaries. The original handoff is preserved; the replacement estimates derive from the original PAD-US polygons and explicit public ownership/access screening. Private/NGO ownership, military land, and Restricted/Closed access are excluded. Percentages use the whole projected county, including water. Missing inventory coverage is not evidence of no outdoor opportunity. Runtime data and source fingerprints are in `src/cole/features/greenspace/data/`; detailed methods, repairs and lineage are in `features/greenspace/`. COLE does not claim USGS or Census Bureau endorsement.
